How each number is calculated

Conversion funnel

Step conversion between each stage, plus end-to-end registration to first trade.

REG→Submit = Submit / REG Submit→L3 = L3 / Submit L3→NFC = NFC / L3 REG→NFC = NFC / REG

CPA

Fully-loaded acquisition cost per new first-trade customer. Marketing spend is an input you supply per market per month.

CPA = Marketing spend / NFC

Revenue per customer

Month-one revenue from the cohort acquired that month.

Revenue per NFC = New total revenue / NFC Gross profit per NFC = Revenue per NFC × Gross margin %

CAC payback

Months of gross profit from one customer needed to repay their acquisition cost. Target is 12 months or better.

Payback (months) = CPA / (Revenue per NFC × Gross margin %)

Net LTV

Value of one customer over 18 months, after the cost of acquiring them. Only as good as the revenue-per-customer input: where that is month-one new-cohort revenue, a customer who grows into real volume is badly understated. Set active_customers per market to switch it onto whole-book revenue.

Net LTV = (N × avg monthly revenue per customer) − CPA N = 18 months avg revenue per customer, in order of preference: 1. total revenue ÷ measured active customers 2. total revenue ÷ estimated active base 3. total revenue ÷ total NFC in the period 4. month-1 new-cohort revenue

Health score

Weighted 0–100 blend. Each component scores 100 when it hits its benchmark; components with no data are dropped and the rest re-weighted, so a market is never penalised for data you have not supplied yet.

Paste monthly data

Copy the rows straight out of the working sheet (including the header row) and paste below. Tab-separated or CSV both work. Existing rows for the same month and market are replaced; every other month is left alone.

Required columns: month, market. Optional: reg, submit, l3, nfc, new_total_revenue, new_transaction_revenue, marketing_spend.
